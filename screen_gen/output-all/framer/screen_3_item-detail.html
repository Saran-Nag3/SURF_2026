New customer summary cards

A refreshed mobile layout for testimonials and short customer notes.

Published Jun 4Product update4 min read

This update brings a simpler card style for customer stories using rounded containers, clearer names, and shorter quotes that are easier to scan on small screens.

We also tightened spacing, improved avatar presentation, and made action buttons more consistent with the rest of the product so new stories feel easier to publish and review.

Included in this release

Integration ecosystemCustomer cards now sit more cleanly beside other product sections.
Lock controlsDraft stories can stay private until they are ready to publish.
NotificationsOwners receive a note when a new story is added or edited.
Next up: permission settings and smaller improvements for the help flow continue below the fold.
