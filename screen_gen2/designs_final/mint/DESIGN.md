# mint - Design System

## Overview

- Mobile food-app design source covering UX, UI, and Extra pages.
- Primary visual direction: food/community product with green brand accents, white surfaces, dark text, rounded cards, and rating/social-proof elements.
- No local variable collections found.
- Most-used named styles: `primary` (684 uses), `Primary` (310), `Neutral/True White` (287), and `Card Shadow` (159).

## Design language

- Use white surfaces with dark navy or near-black text.
- Use green as the dominant brand accent:
  - `#5DB329` — `primary`, 684 uses.
  - `#1DAB87` — `Primary/Base`.
  - `#5DB075` — `Green/Primary`.
  - `#4B9460` — `Green/Secondary`.
- Use Poppins most often for product content, with Inter, Sofia Pro, Open Sans, and other source fonts appearing in supporting or raw styles.
- Use rounded cards and bordered content panels for persona and information layouts.
- Use rating components and social-platform logos for reviews and trust signals.
- Keep visual hierarchy spacious and content-led; the source uses large headings, 16–20px body copy, and compact 10–14px UI labels.

- UX page includes persona layouts for:
  - Customer persona.
  - Kitchen-owner persona.
- Each persona screen is a `1080x1214px` frame with a white background.
- Repeated content pattern:
  - Profile card: `360x650px`, radius `10px`, white fill, image area `300x300px`, identity metadata, and divider line.
  - Bio card: `540x271px`, radius `10px`.
  - Goals and needs card: `540x196px`, radius `10px`.
  - Pain-point card: `540x196px`, radius `10px`.
  - App-help card: `360x196px`, radius `10px`.
  - Interests card: `360x196px`, radius `10px`.
  - Personality chart: `540x356px`, with six horizontal slider-style rows and 20px control markers.
- Persona card headings use Poppins Regular `24px`; body content uses Poppins Regular `16px` or `20px`.
- Persona cards use white surfaces, green heading accents in the source, dark body copy, thin borders, and rounded corners.
- Customer content focuses on ordering, tracking, reviews/ratings, homemade meals, convenience, and flexible ordering.
- Kitchen-owner content focuses on efficiency, sales, business growth, visibility, reporting, inventory, compliance, and flexible ordering.

## Notes for implementers

- Prefer the named primary green `#5DB329` when a single brand color is required; it is the most-used named primary style.
- Use `#FFFFFF` for dominant surfaces and component fills.
- Use `#333333`, `#666666`, `#121212`, or `#0A2533` for dark text according to hierarchy.
- Use Poppins for the main product UI unless a specific component calls for Inter or Sofia Pro.
- Do not assume spacing or radius variables exist; use only the observed component values.
- Keep buttons and form controls at their specified fixed sizes.
- Preserve the exact misspelling `Email adress` when referring to the source component family.
- Use the provided rating states and platform-specific star dimensions rather than substituting a generic rating component.
- Use the supplied shadow styles only for cards, hover elements, and button-content hover states.
