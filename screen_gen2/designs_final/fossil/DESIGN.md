# fossil - Design System

## Overview

Single-page luxury watch landing-page concept with a dark circular hero composition, pink accent elements, oversized product typography, minimal navigation, social links, and a prominent add-to-cart action. Source includes a thumbnail, primary landing page, icon assets, and a product image.

## Design language

- Luxury editorial direction with large, high-contrast typography.
- Dark hero background paired with white type and pink accent lines, circles, and controls.
- Circular and elliptical forms frame the watch product imagery.
- Navigation is minimal and icon-led.
- Product messaging uses oversized display text and vertical labels.
- Primary action uses a white rounded rectangle with dark text.
- Decorative oversized “WATCH” typography appears as a low-contrast background element.

## Color palette

Ranked by source usage:

1. `#FFFFFF` — 10 uses. Primary light surface and text color.
2. `#8B8B8B` — 4 uses. Secondary numeric navigation text.
3. `#F19695` — 3 uses. Accent color for circular elements and divider lines.

## Spacing scale

- `0px` — frame padding in the icon navigation groups.
- `57px` — gap between navigation and social icons.

## Radius scale

- `6px` — add-to-cart button and its containing group.
- `30px` — primary landing-page frame.
- `0px` — explicit radius values on grouped numeric navigation elements.

- Drop shadow with `27.7px` offset applied to the thumbnail’s central image.

- Drop shadow with `40.8px` offset applied to the smaller accent circle.
- Background blur of `15px` applied to the translucent thumbnail ellipse.

## Screen patterns

- **Thumbnail / Thumb** — `1920x1080px`. Centered landing-page preview with a circular translucent overlay and watch imagery.
- **Design / Landing Page** — `1940x1000px`. Primary hero screen with icon navigation, social links, gender navigation, circular product framing, oversized product copy, active slide numbering, add-to-cart action, and decorative vertical typography.
- **Icons** — Individual `50x50px` assets for menu, search, user, grocery store, Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, and mouse controls. The landing page uses these assets at `30x30px`, except the standalone mouse asset at `50x50px`.
- **Images** — Product image asset displayed at `734x734px`.

## Notes for implementers

- Preserve the dark, editorial hero composition and strong contrast between white typography and the dark visual field.
- Use `#F19695` selectively for circular accents and divider lines.
- Keep the active slide number substantially larger than the inactive numbers.
- Treat “FOSSIL” and “Super Luxury” as oversized hero hierarchy, with the descriptive copy beneath them.
- Use the white rounded add-to-cart control as the primary conversion element.
- Keep navigation and social controls icon-based and spatially separated.
- No spacing, radius, typography, color, component, grid, or effect variables were defined locally in the source.
