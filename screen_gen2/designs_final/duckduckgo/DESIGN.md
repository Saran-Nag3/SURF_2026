# duckduckgo - Design System

## Overview

Landing-page UI extracted from the DuckDuckGo Community Figma file, Page 1. The design uses Open Sans as its primary typeface, with Inter also present, a white-dominant palette, dark text, blue accents, and vivid multi-color gradients.

## Design language

- Primary typeface: Open Sans.
- Secondary typeface: Inter.
- Typography uses Regular 400 and Bold 700 weights; one raw SemiBold 600 style also appears.
- Large display headings use Open Sans Bold at approximately 56.62–58.62px with 64px line height and -1.92px tracking.
- Body copy primarily uses Open Sans Regular at approximately 13–19px with 20–28px line heights.
- Uppercase labels use 14px / 20px or 14px / 14px with 2.1px tracking.
- The palette is predominantly white and dark neutral tones, supported by blue, purple, orange, yellow, green, and red accents.
- Gradients are used for expressive visual emphasis.

## Notes for implementers

- Use only the listed colors and gradient stops.
- Prefer `#FFFFFF` for dominant light surfaces.
- Use `#110336`, `#230545`, `#222222`, `#333333`, `#111111`, or `#1C1C1C` for dark text and dark surfaces according to the extracted design context.
- Use Open Sans for the majority of UI text and headings.
- Preserve the exact line heights and tracking values when applying the listed type styles.
- Use the large Open Sans Bold styles for display headings; do not substitute unlisted sizes.
- No spacing, radius, motion, elevation, component, or grid tokens were extracted.
