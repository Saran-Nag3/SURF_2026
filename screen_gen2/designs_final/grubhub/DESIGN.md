## Design language

- Mobile-first food delivery experience with light-theme screens documented in the extraction.
- Core visual language: white backgrounds, dark green primary text, orange action controls, green active states, and muted gray body text.
- Walkthrough screens use large decorative illustrations, centered pagination indicators, and full-width primary CTAs.
- Authentication screens use compact top bars, lightweight headings, underlined form fields, and social sign-in actions.
- Decorative illustrations and branding are prominent in onboarding screens.

## Notes for implementers

- Use `#010F07` for primary dark text and `#868686` for body copy unless a screen-specific color is specified.
- Use `#EEA734` for the primary CTA fill; the extraction also identifies `#F8B64C` as the accent color.
- Keep mobile CTA width at 335px and height at 48px, with an 8px radius.
- Preserve the 375x812px screen frame and 44px device status-bar region for mobile compositions.
- Walkthrough pagination consists of three 8x5px indicators with 100px radius.
- Use white text on primary CTA fills.
- Use `#F3F2F2` for inactive form dividers and `#868686` for the active form divider.
- No spacing variables, motion tokens, local effect styles, or reusable component families were extracted.
