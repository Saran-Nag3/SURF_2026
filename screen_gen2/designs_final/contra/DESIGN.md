# contra - Design System

## Overview

- Source: Figma file `contra wireframe kit (Community)`.
- Source pages: `Screens`, `Components and Styles`, `Cover`.
- Extracted: 2026-07-25.
- No local variable collections, spacing variables, radius variables, motion variables, or local grid styles were found.
- Primary UI foundation: Montserrat typography, white surfaces, black 2px strokes, rounded 16px cards and controls, and bold color accents.

## Design language

- Use Montserrat throughout.
- Typography is predominantly Medium (500), Bold (700), and ExtraBold (800).
- Headings use ExtraBold or Bold weights, with sizes from 24px to 44px in the defined styles.
- Large titles use 44px line-height 54px or 36px line-height 40px.
- Common body and control text uses 13px–21px.
- UI surfaces are commonly white with black 2px strokes.
- Primary accent colors are Contra Blue, Green, Yellow, and Red.
- Cards, modals, inputs, and buttons commonly use a 16px radius.
- Layouts use fixed mobile-oriented widths, especially 327px and 375px.
- Shadows are solid black drop shadows with vertical offsets.
