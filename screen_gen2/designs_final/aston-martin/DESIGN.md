# aston-martin - Design System

## Overview

A dark Aston Martin CarPlay Ultra presentation and dashboard system spanning case-study covers, product UI, typography, color, user insights, and automotive data concepts. The visual system combines near-black surfaces, pale blue-green text, teal gradients, white iconography, glass panels, and large rounded typography.

## Design language

- **Base:** Dark, cinematic layouts using #000304, #080808, #0D0D0D, #181818, and #1A1A1A.
- **Primary accent:** Teal gradients from #00655F to #003C38.
- **Text:** Pale blue-green #D0DFDD and white #FFFFFF, with #E7EFEE used as a light surface/accent.
- **Interaction/accent color:** Bloody Mary red #BB0103; alert red #FF3B30.
- **Surfaces:** Translucent black overlays, glass panels, gradient image overlays, and subtle blur/shadow effects.
- **Typography:** Manrope is the primary contemporary interface and case-study font. SF Pro Rounded and SF Pro provide large display treatments; Inter supports small utility text.
- **Shape language:** Rounded cards and controls, with observed 16px, 24px, and 48px corner radii.
- **Composition:** Oversized headings, sparse editorial layouts, image-led sections, timeline diagrams, data visualizations, and automotive feature icons.

## Screen patterns

- **Case-study cover:** Full-width dark image composition with Aston Martin branding, large translucent/glass typography, white logo treatments, and dark gradient overlays.
- **Overview section:** Dark #000304 canvas with large Manrope SemiBold headings in #D0DFDD and supporting Manrope Medium copy.
- **Problem cards:** Three glass cards, each 700px wide, with 16px radius, #00000033 fill, teal gradient label areas, and pale blue-green text.
- **Service statement:** Oversized Manrope SemiBold statement over a dark image and layered #000304 gradients.
- **Project timeline:** Large “Project Timeline” heading, supporting copy, pale blue-green timeline strokes, and five numbered stages: Discover, Research, Ideating, Design, Iteration.
- **Typography and color showcase:** Large Manrope specimen heading, weight cards for Light, Regular, Medium, and SemiBold, followed by named color swatches.
- **User insights:** Pie-chart visualizations using #00655F and #003C38, paired with concise insight statements and dark image panels.
- **Product/mockup presentation:** Rounded device or product mockups placed over dark backgrounds with image overlays and large editorial headings.
- **Home Screen/dashboard:** Automotive data-oriented interface using white icons, dark surfaces, teal accents, and compact navigation controls.

## Notes for implementers

- Use only the documented colors; do not introduce additional neutrals or accent colors.
- Prefer #000304 as the primary page background for case-study and presentation surfaces.
- Use #D0DFDD for major headings and body copy on dark backgrounds.
- Use #00655F to #003C38 gradients for active labels, feature emphasis, and branded controls.
- Use #FFFFFF for icons, logos, and high-contrast labels.
- Apply glass treatment sparingly but consistently; it is the dominant effect style in the source.
- Favor Manrope for new interface and editorial content. Use SF Pro Rounded for large display treatments and Inter for small utility text.
- Preserve the contrast between oversized headings and compact supporting copy.
- Use the documented component families only.
- No local variables, text styles, spacing styles, radius styles, motion tokens, or grid styles were found.
