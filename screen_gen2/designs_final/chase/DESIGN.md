# chase - Design System

## Overview

- Community Figma design system focused on inclusive, human-centered design practices.
- Source pages: Thumbnail; We Acknowledge; Shared Language; Shared Language Components; Be Vulnerable; More Inclusivity; Speak Nonviolently; Speak Nonviolently Statements Components; Evaluate Self; White Supremacy Design; How To Aplogize; How Colorblind User Research Produces Racist Design; Co- Creation Credit.
- No local variable collections found.
- No spacing, radius, motion, grid, or local effect style collections found.

## Design language

- Visual language combines high-contrast editorial layouts with inclusive-design educational content.
- Primary presentation uses white and black, with vivid accent colors across the spectrum.
- Typography is primarily Atkinson Hyperlegible for large editorial and accessibility-focused content.
- Supporting typography includes FreightSans Pro, Inter, Archivo, Poppins, Roboto, Vollkorn, and SF Pro Text.
- Cards and educational definition components use rounded corners, compact padding, and readable text blocks.
- The Thumbnail uses a multicolor linear gradient heading from #EB00FF through #8A3FFC, #0F62FE, #08BDBA, #24A148, #F1C21B to #FA4D56.

## Notes for implementers

- Prefer Atkinson Hyperlegible for primary accessible reading and editorial content.
- Preserve the strong black-and-white contrast and use saturated accent colors sparingly for categorization and emphasis.
- Use 8px radius for definition cards and document surfaces; do not add shadows because none are specified.
- Definition cards are fixed at 564px wide with 12px padding and 10px internal gap by default.
- Preserve transparent fills where specified rather than replacing them with opaque accent colors.
- Use 612×792px for Inclusive Design document pages.
- Use 500×70px for Accordion instances and 22.29×22.29px for Checkmark instances.
- Do not introduce spacing, radius, motion, elevation, or effect tokens that are not listed in this document.
