# reflect - Design System

## Overview

- Dark, premium AI SaaS marketing website for Reflect.
- Primary canvas: `#030014`.
- Visual language combines white and lavender typography, purple borders, translucent surfaces, blurred glows, star-light effects, connected-note graphics, and large decorative imagery.
- Source page: `Page 1 / reflect.app`, a `1440x13460px` frame. Thumbnail page contains no extracted design-system data.

## Design language

- Use a near-black violet background with luminous lavender and white accents.
- Headings use Roboto Medium and large sizes: `72px`, `56px`, and `32px`.
- Body and interface text use Inter at `12px`, `13px`, `14px`, `15px`, `16px`, and `18px`.
- Headings commonly use white-to-translucent-white gradients.
- Supporting text uses muted lavender-white values such as `#EFEDFD99` and `#EFEDFDB2`.
- Pills and badges use rounded `32px` corners, subtle translucent fills, and `1px` purple borders.
- Buttons use `8px` radius, purple translucent backgrounds, and luminous inner effects.
- Decorative visuals include star fields, black-hole imagery, graph-like note connections, radial glows, blurred light streaks, and image-based hero artwork.
- Content is centered within wide `1440px` sections, with major feature content commonly constrained to `900px`, `936px`, or `1216px`.

## Notes for implementers

- Preserve the dark violet canvas with `#030014`; do not replace it with neutral black.
- Use only the listed palette values for solid colors and translucent surfaces.
- Use Roboto Medium for major headings and Inter for body/interface text.
- Keep headings centered and visually dominant.
- Treat badges as luminous, rounded pills with `32px` radius and `1px` purple borders.
- Treat the trial CTA as a distinct rounded button with `8px` radius.
- Build feature areas around centered, wide compositions rather than dense layouts.
- Use blur, translucent borders, radial gradients, and image-backed decoration to create depth.
- Decorative graph, star, black-hole, and hero imagery is important to the visual identity; do not flatten these areas into plain color blocks.
